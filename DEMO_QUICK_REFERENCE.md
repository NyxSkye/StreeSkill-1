## 🚀 Quick Start Command
```bash
flutter run
```

## 📱 Demo Flow (2 Minutes)

### 1. Splash Screen (0:00-0:03)
- **What to say**: "StreeSkill opens with our branded splash screen"
- **What happens**: Animated logo and tagline appear
- **Wait for**: Auto-navigation to home (3 seconds)

### 2. Home Dashboard (0:03-0:30)
- **What to say**: "The home dashboard shows personalized impact metrics"
- **Point out**:
  - Welcome message with user name
  - Impact card with 4 metrics (courses, hours, certificates, skill score)
  - Progress bar showing 78.5% skill score
  - Continue Learning horizontal scroll
  - Trending courses section
- **Action**: Scroll down slowly to show all sections

### 3. Courses Tab (0:30-0:50)
- **What to say**: "Browse 100+ courses across multiple categories"
- **Action**: Tap "Courses" in bottom navigation
- **Point out**:
  - Category filter chips at top
  - Grid layout of course cards
- **Action**: Tap "Technology" filter to show filtering
- **Action**: Tap any course card

### 4. Course Detail (0:50-1:20)
- **What to say**: "Each course has comprehensive information and video preview"
- **Point out**:
  - Video player at top
  - Instructor profile
  - Stats (rating, students, duration)
  - Course description
  - Module breakdown
- **Action**: Tap play button on video (let it play 3-5 seconds)
- **Action**: Scroll down to show modules
- **Action**: Tap back button

### 5. Community Tab (1:20-1:45)
- **What to say**: "Active community with 12,500+ members sharing success stories"
- **Action**: Tap "Community" in bottom navigation
- **Point out**:
  - Community stats banner (members, active today, posts)
  - Social feed with posts
  - Like, comment, share buttons
- **Action**: Scroll through 2-3 posts

### 6. Profile Tab (1:45-2:00)
- **What to say**: "Gamified profile with achievements and progress tracking"
- **Action**: Tap "Profile" in bottom navigation
- **Point out**:
  - User profile header
  - Achievement badges (4 badges)
  - Completed courses list
  - Settings options
- **Action**: Scroll to show all sections

### 7. Closing (2:00)
- **What to say**: "StreeSkill - empowering women through accessible education"
- **Action**: Return to home screen
